### Profile

Geospatial Data and GIS Analyst with 6+ years of experience managing spatial data, developing ESRI mapping applications, and preparing forest and land-resource data to government standards. I build ArcGIS and Python/R workflows to process multi-platform LiDAR and multispectral datasets, maintain and quality-control enterprise geodatabases (PostgreSQL/PostGIS), and turn spatial data into maps, dashboards, and decision-support tools.

I also apply machine and deep learning (U-Net) to land use / land cover mapping and individual-tree detection, so analysis scales from a single stand to national coverage.

* **GIS & Mapping:** ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Enterprise/Online, QGIS; cartography, geoprocessing, spatial analysis
* **Spatial Data:** enterprise geodatabase design, QA/QC, and maintenance (PostgreSQL/PostGIS); data standards and exchange
* **Remote Sensing:** LiDAR (terrestrial, mobile, UAV), multispectral and satellite imagery; land-cover classification and change detection
* **Automation:** reproducible Python/R geospatial pipelines and workflows (Git)

### Featured Work

#### [National Forest Inventory Dashboard](https://tuoe.me/)
*Geospatial Data Analyst — General Directorate of Forestry*  
A GIS decision-support web application for Turkey's National Forest Inventory, serving 100+ users.

- Enterprise geodatabase (PostgreSQL) design, maintenance, and data QA/QC
- Dynamic R Shiny application with interactive maps and charts
- Proximal-sensing data ingestion feeding national-scale reporting

#### [ISCL: Individual Tree Detection from LiDAR](https://event.fourwaves.com/silvilaser2025/abstracts/fc64cafb-c8ed-43ee-ab46-f28f50bbf9d6/)
*Research method (in development, 2025) — presented at SilviLaser 2025, Québec City*  
Watershed and CHM-based methods struggle under overlapping crowns and multi-story canopies. ISCL detects individual trees using vertical density signatures through inter-slice clustering and geometric graph fitting rather than surface-based methods.

*Stack:* Python (NumPy/SciPy) and R.
